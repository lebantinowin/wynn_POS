# Application foundation

Laravel 13 and Livewire 4 with a database-free sample catalogue. MySQL is the planned database; it has not been set up and no migrations have run. Sessions/cache use files for the preview. No Node build is required for the current plain CSS interface.

## Run locally

Run `./run-preview.ps1` from PowerShell, then open http://127.0.0.1:8000. PHP with mbstring and fileinfo is required. Composer dependencies are installed and locked in composer.lock.

## Working preview behavior

Interface styling uses locally hosted Inter (license included in public/fonts), white/slate surfaces, and muted green accents. Navigation has consistent line icons, current-page indication, and a tablet drawer with backdrop/Escape dismissal. Purchasing forms are opened on demand. Desktop, tablet and mobile layouts were visually inspected; purchasing and checkout regression checks passed after the redesign.

Shared motion uses 180–340 ms easing for controls, the sidebar drawer, overlay, forms and receipt previews. Livewire navigation transitions the content region while the sidebar remains stationary. Closed tablet navigation is inert, and opening it locks background scrolling. Reduced-motion preferences disable animations. Navigation, supplier form interaction after navigation, drawer opening/closing and restored scrolling were checked in the browser.

Checkout displays image placeholder cards and thumbnails in cart lines; catalogue entries also display thumbnails. Selected product cards show an In cart base-unit count and per-option quantities, including package selections. Real product photos and upload management are deferred. The placeholder is a local SVG, so it needs no external image service. Browser inspection confirmed the thumbnail/highlight after adding a pack; 15 affected feature tests passed.

Sidebar navigation, responsive layouts, sample catalogue, name/SKU search, sample barcode lookup on Enter, repeated scan increments, individual/package quantities, item removal, cash coverage validation, manual GCash/Maya confirmation, and receipt preview. Prices are illustrative; currency/tax configuration is pending.

Catalogue creation/editing, package options, reorder thresholds, expiry tracking with opening-stock dates, and manual stock counts now use temporary Laravel session storage. They are shared with checkout in the same browser session, survive ordinary page navigation/reloads while that session remains valid, and are not shared across devices or backed up. Stock counts require a reason and keep before/after quantities and differences in session history. Existing package conversions are fixed. Prices accept at most two decimal places and barcodes/SKUs must be unique. Opening stock and later deliveries keep separate expiry dates. Remaining quantities per batch, expiry-based sale blocking and automatic batch depletion remain pending.

Completed sales and payment details now use session storage. Completing a sale validates the cart/payment, aggregates base-unit deductions across individual/package lines, blocks insufficient stock, and stores a numbered receipt with immutable snapshots. Sequential retries with the same submission identifier return the existing sale. Sale history supports receipt/product/date text search and payment filters; copies are marked COPY and do not alter stock. Browser Print/Save PDF prints the selected receipt. Physical thermal-printer integration remains pending. New sale lines preserve the configured purchase cost per base unit and its extended individual/package cost in cents. Later product edits and retries preserve completed snapshots. Missing costs remain null. These are estimates; supplier delivery costs do not automatically update product costs, and actual batch allocation remains pending.

Customer Credit & Loans supports customer creation, grocery credit through checkout, cash-loan principal records, optional due dates, and partial/full repayments. Credit sale completion saves the sale, stock deduction, and linked debt together in the session. Repayments never create sales or deduct stock; overpayments are blocked. Cash loans record money already lent and do not execute transfers. Submission identifiers prevent sequential duplicate debt/repayment events. Interest and general debt edits/write-offs remain pending. Grocery-sale returns reduce the remaining debt and refund only any paid excess through Transactions.

Stock Losses records unpaid consumption, damage, expiry, missing stock, or other reasons. Individual/package quantities deduct shared base-unit stock, with notes and before/after history. Optional manually entered acquisition cost is valued separately; unknown cost stays null, and zero cost is a known zero. Losses do not create sales or customer debt. The interface directs recoverable customer credit to checkout to avoid duplicate deductions.

Shifts & Expenses supports opening cash (including zero), one open session per preview workspace, reasoned cash additions/withdrawals, paid operating expenses, closing counts, and signed shortages/surpluses. A difference requires explanatory closing notes. Closed totals are preserved and can be reviewed or printed through the browser. All new completed sales require an open session; cash sales contribute their total after change, and grocery credit does not add drawer money. Cash loans, repayments and expenses require an open drawer and save their source record and one linked movement together. Cash outflows cannot exceed expected drawer cash. GCash/Maya amounts are separate; digital expenses/loans/repayments recorded without a session remain outside drawer history and are not reassigned later. Earlier preview sales without a cash-session link are excluded from drawer totals. Repeat submission identifiers avoid sequential duplicates; older tabs bound to a previous session are rejected until reloaded.

Dashboard and Reports now show session-derived sales/collections trends, payment breakdowns, top products, customer receivables/overdue balances, loans given, low stock, losses by reason, expenses by category, and cash-session shortages/surpluses. Filters cover the last 7/30/90 local calendar days in Asia/Shanghai; current receivables and stock are explicitly all-time/current. Repayments are collections, cash loans are excluded from sales, and missing loss costs are flagged. Daily chart values are also accessible in a table. Estimated cost of goods, gross profit, gross margin, operating profit and per-product gross profit now use saved sale costs. Overall totals are withheld for missing sale costs; unknown stock-loss cost also withholds operating profit. Costed revenue and partial profit coverage are disclosed. Paid expenses and stock losses reduce operating profit; loans, repayments, cash-session differences and taxes are excluded.

Owner authentication, MySQL persistence, offline storage/sync, physical printer integration, and delivery-batch cost allocation remain future milestones. Session storage is not a production transaction store, backup, or cross-device system. Receipt numbering is local to a session; currency/tax/fiscal requirements remain unconfigured and receipts are marked accordingly. Mobile analytics-only behavior is currently a responsive layout rule; device policy is not enforced by the backend yet. Unimplemented modules display explicit placeholders.

## Checks

Current design stage is approved complete for now; later revisions are allowed. Latest full suite: 81 tests and 823 assertions passed; Blade compilation and Pint passed. New cash tests cover opening/closing, one active session, exact cash after change, automatic loans/repayments/expense entries, negative-drawer prevention, reasoned differences, sequential duplicate events, stale tabs, preserved closing snapshots, and digital entries outside drawer sessions. Earlier tests cover credit, losses, date windows, stock, receipt snapshots, and purchasing. Browser checks covered opening → sale → manual addition → expense → closing with a shortage, tablet closing-history navigation, and mobile expense/difference reports with cash operations hidden. Demo entries remain in the preview session. Next development milestone is supplier balances and payment records.

Purchasing supports supplier creation/editing and multi-line purchase orders in temporary session storage. Orders retain product/package and expected-cost snapshots. Partial/full receiving records actual costs and expiry, updates base-unit stock and delivery history, and tracks remaining order quantities. All delivery lines are validated before committing session changes. Delivery event identifiers prevent repeated submissions from adding stock twice. No orders are emailed or otherwise sent to suppliers; supplier balances/payments remain pending. Laravel Boost was added as a development dependency under repository guidance.

Inventory now supports session delivery receiving by individual/package option, whole quantities, purchase cost per selected option, supplier name, optional invoice reference and expiry date. Deliveries update base-unit stock and keep before/after balances and total cost in session history. Expiry-tracked products require a valid non-expired date. This does not implement batch depletion, profit costing, supplier invoices/balances or payment. Current suite: 13 tests, 96 assertions passed.

Transactions now exposes sale and return/refund history with receipt/product/customer/local-date search, payment filters, all/7/30/90-day periods, return status, original receipt reprints and return-record printing. Loans/repayments and drawer movements remain in their dedicated histories. Existing receipts without customer/cost fields are supported without rewriting them.

Returns use whole quantities of the original sale option (including whole packages), original selling prices and saved purchase costs. Sellable items restore base stock; discarded items create one linked loss without another stock deduction. Credit returns reduce the outstanding grocery debt first, then refund any paid excess. Money refunds require owner confirmation and an open cash session; cash refunds cannot overdraw the expected drawer. A sequential retry identifier prevents repeated refund/stock/debt records, stale cash-session forms are rejected, and all session changes are staged before one write. Original receipts and closed cash summaries remain unchanged. New return events reduce net sales and collections on their recorded date; cost reversals and discarded loss costs reconcile estimated profit. Charts support negative net totals. Real payment transfers, MySQL concurrency protection and delivery-batch allocation remain pending.
